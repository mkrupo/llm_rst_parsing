### Antithesis
- **N:** W identifies positively with N.
- **S:** W rejects or gives less importance to S.
- **N/S:** The spans are incompatible; recognizing this increases R's positive regard for N.
- **Typical connectives:** but; rather; instead.

### Background
- **N/S:** Understanding S makes it easier for R to understand N by supplying prior or general information.
- **Effect:** R's ability to comprehend N is increased.
- **Remark:** Background often connects a larger introductory segment to N.

### Circumstance
- **S:** A temporal, spatial, situational, or interpretive framework.
- **N/S:** N is interpreted within the framework in S.
- **Typical connectives:** when; while; as.

### Concession
- **S:** W acknowledges S although it appears incompatible with N.
- **N/S:** N still holds and is more central.
- **Typical connectives:** although; but; still; despite.

### Condition
- **S:** A hypothetical, future, or unrealized situation.
- **N/S:** Realization of N depends on realization of S.
- **Typical connectives:** if; in case.

### Elaboration
- **N/S:** S adds detail about N or an element of N: member, instance, part, step, attribute, or specific detail.
- **Effect:** R recognizes the additional detail.

### Enablement
- **N:** An action to be performed by R.
- **N/S:** S increases R's ability to perform N.

### Evaluation
- **N/S:** S evaluates, assesses, or appraises N.
- **Effect:** R recognizes the assessment.

### Evidence
- **N:** A claim R may not believe strongly enough.
- **N/S:** S increases R's belief that N is true.

### Interpretation
- **N/S:** S interprets the meaning or significance of N through a broader framework.
- **Remark:** Prefer Evaluation when appraisal is primary.

### Justify
- **N/S:** S increases R's readiness to accept W's right, basis, or authority to present N.
- **Remark:** This supports the act of asserting rather than primarily proving N true.

### Motivation
- **N:** An action to be performed by R.
- **N/S:** S increases R's desire to perform N.

### Non-volitional Cause
- **N/S:** S non-deliberately causes N.
- **Effect:** R recognizes S as the cause of N.

### Non-volitional Result
- **N/S:** S is a non-deliberate result caused by N.
- **Effect:** R recognizes N as the cause of S.

### Otherwise
- **S:** An unrealized situation.
- **N/S:** N depends on the non-realization of S.

### Purpose
- **N:** An activity or action.
- **S:** The intended goal realized through N.

### Restatement
- **N/S:** S repeats N in different words at roughly equal scope.

### Solutionhood
- **S:** A problem, question, request, or need.
- **N/S:** N provides or contributes to a solution.

### Summary
- **N:** More than one EDU.
- **N/S:** S gives a shorter condensation of N.

### Volitional Cause
- **N/S:** S causes or motivates an intentional action in N.

### Volitional Result
- **N/S:** S is an intentional response or result caused by N.

### Preparation
- **N/S:** S precedes N and readies or orients R toward N.
- **Remark:** Preparation shapes expectations; Background supplies substantive knowledge.

### Contrast
- **N:** Exactly two equal-weight nuclei are comparable but differ in important respects.

### Joint
- **N:** Equal-weight spans are coherently connected without a more specific relation.

### List
- **N:** Equal-weight comparable items form a list or set.

### Sequence
- **N:** Equal-weight events or steps occur in temporal or procedural order.

Use `NS` when unit 1 is nucleus, `SN` when unit 2 is nucleus, and `NN` for multinuclear relations. Distinguish Evidence from Justify, Antithesis from Concession and Contrast, Background from Circumstance and Preparation, cause from result, volitional from non-volitional causality, and Restatement from Summary.
